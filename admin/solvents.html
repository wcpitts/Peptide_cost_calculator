Hematian Lab solvent policy

DMF and pyrrolidine must be supplied by the Hematian Lab for all Liberty Blue 2.0 syntheses. Requester-supplied DMF or pyrrolidine is not permitted. Amino acids may be requester-supplied only after approval.
